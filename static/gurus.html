Gurus Lab

Each guru lane copies the investor's latest 13F filing: their 25 biggest US stock positions, weighted by reported value, bought at the close on the first trading day 46 days after the quarter ends (the day the filing is public — never earlier). Returns include dividends, 5bp per trade, $10,000 per lane. History before launch is a point-in-time backtest of the same rule; from then on it runs forward as paper.

Read with care: a 13F shows only US-listed long stocks — no shorts, options, bonds, cash or foreign shares — and is up to 4½ months old, so a copy is not the fund's real return. Stock Rover lists are tracked forward only from the day you paste them (a screen run backwards is hindsight) and stay private to this lab.
