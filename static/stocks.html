Rules (frozen for the test window)

    

      
MOM — the scanner's momentum factor with the platform's two-phase exit engine.
        Buy when EXPL (1m+3m momentum) is in the top decile of the 80 stocks, price > SMA50 > SMA200, and the 3-day return ≥ 0.
        Double down once when 1m ≥ +10%, 3d ≥ 0 and price is within 3% of the peak.
        Phase 1 exits at −15% from entry. Phase 2 exits 15% off the peak. Going nowhere (28 days, ±5%) → exit.

      
PB90 — the Slope PULLBACK lane. EXPL rank ≥ 75, 1-year rank ≥ 70, above SMA200, 2–12% under the 63-session high, with the bounce started (3d > 0).
        Stop at 2×ATR. Target is a retest of the high. Time stop after 20 sessions.

      
RSI2 — Connors mean reversion on the 20 ETFs. Buy when above SMA200 and RSI(2) < 10.
        Exit at 15:50 once price is above the 5-day SMA, or after 10 sessions. Catastrophe stop at 3×ATR.

      
Sizing: $500 risk per campaign (qty × stop distance), max 8% notional. Portfolio heat ≤ 8%. New risk ≤ 2% per day.
        Fills are simulated at the live quote ± 5 bps (stocks) / 2 bps (ETFs), rounded against us, with SEC/TAF fees.
        Splits are healed. Dividends are not credited.
